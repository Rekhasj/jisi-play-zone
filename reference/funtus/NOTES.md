## Site structure

Menu: Home · About Us · Attractions · Membership · Pricing · Birthday Packages ·
Gallery · Testimonials · Contact Us · Book Now (purple button).
Floating WhatsApp button (bottom-left) and purple back-to-top button (bottom-right).

Home page order:
1. Purple top bar (phone, email, social icons) + white header with rainbow logo
2. Photo slider (play area + birthday stage)
3. "Why choose us" — 4 pastel cards with cartoon icons (safe, excitement, learning, parents' lounge with Wi-Fi)
4. Yellow "about" block with blob-shaped photo slider
5. Blue banner with scalloped (cloud) bottom edge
6. Attractions cards (interactive games, clay moulds, sandpit, tunnel slides)
7. Light-blue testimonials with cartoon kids illustration
8. Dark navy "space" footer: call-to-action, planet + cloud doodles, quick links, contact, Google map

Colours: purple buttons, yellow and light-blue sections, pastel cards, navy footer.

## Their pricing (Bengaluru mall, for comparison)

Play: ₹200 (1 ride free, socks ₹50 extra) · ₹350 (2 rides) · ₹500 and ₹600 (2 rides, socks free).
The site doesn't say how long each session is or which days each price applies to.

Membership (hours bundle):
- ₹2,000 → 10 hrs, 1 month
- ₹4,900 → 30 hrs, 3 months
- ₹9,900 → 100 hrs, 6 months
- ₹14,900 → 300 hrs, 1 year

Birthday packages:
- **Basic ₹9,900** — stage + balloon decoration, cake up to 2 kg; 2 hrs weekdays / 1 hr weekends; play area charged extra (50% off)
- **Silver ₹19,900** — up to 30 kids (100 people total), play area 2 hrs weekend / 4 hrs weekday, socks free, welcome drinks, cake up to 3 kg
- **Premium ₹39,900** — LED butterfly entry + name board, premium balloons, 30 kids, 3 hrs weekend / 5 hrs weekday, snacks + sweets, cake up to 5 kg, photography with 100 printed photos + album

Policy: no refunds or cancellation; booking can be transferred to family or friends.

## Ideas worth borrowing for JiSi (in our own style)

- Membership sold as **hour bundles** with "per hour" savings shown — easy for parents to compare
- Birthday packages clearly split into **Including / Excluding**
- Different play time for weekday vs weekend parties
- Party booking rules (advance, transfer instead of refund) stated on the page
- Lots of real photos of the birthday stage — add yours once the zone is set up
