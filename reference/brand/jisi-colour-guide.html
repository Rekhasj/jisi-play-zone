One set of colours for the play area, website, T-shirts and posters

Teal and soft yellow with a pop of coral, on clean white. The play equipment is the colourful star; the website, print and uniforms use the same colours more calmly, so parents recognise JiSi everywhere and every photo of the play area looks on-brand.

The palette

Five brand colours, one helper

Give these exact codes to everyone: the play-equipment supplier, the T-shirt printer and the poster designer. HEX is for screens, CMYK is for print.

How much of each

The 60 · 25 · 10 · 5 rule

Most of any space or design is calm white, then teal, then a little yellow, and only a pop of coral. Navy is only for text and logos. Keeping to this mix is what stops a kids’ place from looking messy.

Where the colours go

Same colours, different strength in each place

The play area is the loudest. Everything else steps back so the play area and the kids stay the heroes.

For the equipment supplier

Indoor play area

  • Frame posts and pipes: teal
  • Padded beams, platforms, steps: soft yellow
  • Wave slide and tunnel slide: coral, or one coral and one yellow
  • Safety nets: white (looks clean and bright in photos)
  • Ball pit: equal mix of teal, yellow, coral and white balls, nothing else
  • Floor mats: teal and white checks, navy edging strip
  • Walls and ceiling: cloud white with teal cloud murals; one coral or yellow feature wall in the party room
  • Ride-on car and bikes, sandpit edge: coral and yellow
For the website

Website

  • Header, footer, banners: teal with white text
  • Page background: white and cloud white, so play-area photos pop
  • Main buttons (Book now): soft yellow with navy text
  • Highlights and offers: small coral badges only
  • All text: navy, never pure black
For the T-shirt printer

Staff T-shirts

  • Floor staff: teal T-shirt, white JiSi logo on the chest, “JiSi Play Zone · Kuppam” in navy on the back
  • Party host: soft-yellow T-shirt, so parents can spot the host at a party
  • Collar and sleeve trim: navy, which hides marks
  • Avoid white T-shirts: they stain quickly in a play area
  • Order 2–3 per person so a clean one is always ready
For the print shop

Pamphlets and flyers

  • Paper: white; teal band at the top with the JiSi name
  • Prices: inside a yellow circle (₹200 weekday, ₹300 weekend)
  • Offers: coral sticker shape, one per flyer
  • Phone and WhatsApp: large, on a navy strip at the bottom
  • Ask for a printed proof first: screen colours look brighter than paper
For Instagram and WhatsApp status

Social posts

  • Teal post: news and announcements (opening, holidays)
  • Yellow post: prices, offers and memberships
  • White post with a photo: real moments from the play area and parties
  • Rotate the three so the profile grid looks planned and tidy
  • Same fonts every time, logo in the same corner, phone number on offer posts
For the sign maker

Shop signboard

  • Board: teal with big white “JiSi Play Zone”
  • Strip: yellow with navy text, “Indoor soft play · Birthday parties”
  • Phone number: large enough to read from the road
  • Only these colours, so the board is clear from a distance
Keep it looking good

Simple rules for everyone

Do

  • Keep walls and floors light so the colourful equipment stands out and the room feels bigger
  • Use at most three bright colours in any one spot
  • Use the same two fonts everywhere: Baloo 2 for headings and Nunito for text
  • Show the supplier and printer this page and ask for a physical colour sample before the big order

Avoid

  • The old “every colour of the rainbow” look: it photographs as messy
  • Dark walls or dark floors: the room looks small and photos come out dim
  • Adding purple or extra colours on posters “just for fun”
  • Black text: use navy, it’s softer and matches

CMYK values are close starting points. Printers and equipment makers each have their own colour cards, so ask them to match the HEX swatch and show you a sample. On the website, white text sits on a slightly deeper teal (#0F766E) so it is easy to read.
